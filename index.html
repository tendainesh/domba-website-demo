Welcome to Domba Hospitality

With properties in Bulawayo, Harare, Nyanga, and Lake Kariba, we offer exceptional hospitality experiences across Zimbabwe's most beautiful locations.

Our Story

Founded in 2005, Domba Hospitality has grown from a single lodge in Bulawayo to a premier hospitality group with multiple properties across Zimbabwe. We are a family owned and family run business with very deep routes in our Manyika culture - reflecting our commitment to bringing people together through exceptional service.

We pride ourselves on blending modern comforts with authentic Zimbabwean hospitality, creating memorable experiences for both leisure and business travelers.
